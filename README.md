## 🌟 Overview

**DEEN** is a modern, high-performance anime streaming application designed specifically for enthusiasts who value aesthetic perfection, extensive catalogs, and a buttery smooth viewing experience. 

Built with an unwavering commitment to quality, DEEN blends a striking dark obsidian user interface with responsive touch controls, instant playback, 120Hz display refresh support, and access to an enormous library of anime titles across all eras. Best of all: **minimal, non-intrusive ads that never interrupt your enjoyment, and absolutely no paywalls**.

---

## ✨ Interface & 120Hz Fluid Experience

DEEN was crafted to look and feel like a luxury flagship application:

- **120Hz Hardware Refresh Rate Support**: Automatically unlocks 120Hz/90Hz/144Hz high refresh rate display modes on supported Android devices for silky-smooth 120fps system compositing.
- **Auto-Hiding Top Bar**: Pinned, frosted-glass header with category filter pills that smoothly hides when scrolling down and reappears when scrolling up, with scoped pull-to-refresh.
- **Butter-Smooth Detail Page Transitions**: Zero dropped frames and zero jitter when entering or exiting show detail pages via GPU layer caching and linear flight tweens.
- **Obsidian & Electric Accent Aesthetic**: Designed with deep true-black OLED surfaces accented by electric lime highlights. Optimized for low-light viewing, power efficiency, and minimal eye strain.
- **Frosted Glass Floating Navigation**: Floating pill navigation bar featuring real-time backdrop blur provides intuitive, one-handed navigation across screens.
- **Tactile Micro-Interactions**: Spring-physics interactions, scale animations on button taps, and animated HUD gesture sliders.

## 🎬 Cinema-Grade Gesture Video Player

The media player in DEEN offers full control with natural touch gestures:

- **Full-Screen Immersive Landscape**: Automatically locks to widescreen landscape mode for an edge-to-edge cinematic experience.
- **Fastest Server Engine**: Intelligent concurrent latency probe that auto-selects the lowest latency stream with real-time badges (⚡ Fastest).
- **Universal Generic Server Numbering**: Streamlined server names (`Server 1`, `Server 2`, `Server 3`) across detail, player HUD, and settings screens.
- **1.5x Hold Speed Gesture**: Long-press hold gesture delivers a smooth 1.5x speed boost with automatic migration from legacy settings.
- **Dedicated Screen Lock**: Floating lock control on top bar and video overlay ensuring zero accidental interruptions during playback.
- **Intuitive Edge Swipes**:
  - **Left Edge (Vertical Swipe)**: Adjust screen brightness on the fly.
  - **Right Edge (Vertical Swipe)**: Adjust media volume with precision on-screen HUD indicators.
- **Quick Seeking**: Double-tap on the left or right side of the screen to jump backward or forward by 10 seconds.
- **Sliding Episode Drawer**: Switch to another episode directly inside the player with a sliding drawer without interrupting full-screen playback.
- **Smart Next Episode Countdown**: An interactive card appears in the final moments of an episode to seamlessly autoplay the next episode.
- **Multi-Track Audio & Subtitles**: Easily switch between original Japanese audio, dubbed versions, and multi-language subtitles.
- **Multi-Quality Streaming**: Switch effortlessly between 360p, 480p, 720p, 1080p, or Auto adaptive resolution based on your network speed.
